Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

Carmonela Studio is a free web-based content planning and follower progress tracking tool. This policy describes the current public website. The website does not currently offer TikTok account sign-in or automated TikTok publishing.

Information you provide

If you manually enter follower counts or a follower goal, that information is stored in your browser's local storage on your device. The website does not send those entries to a Carmonela Studio server. You may edit or delete entries through the website or clear your browser's site data.

TikTok information

The current website links to a public TikTok profile. It does not access private TikTok account information, request TikTok credentials, or collect TikTok authorization tokens. If TikTok authorization is added in the future, this policy will be updated before it is made available to users.

Hosting and technical data

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to deliver the site, according to its own policies. The website may load its public daily content plan from GitHub's raw content service. The site does not currently use its own advertising or analytics cookies.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell the follower counts you enter. Your locally saved entries remain on your device until you remove them. GitHub's hosting-related records are governed by GitHub's policies.

Your choices

You can avoid entering follower counts, remove individual records in the app, or clear this site's local storage. Do not enter personal information about another person without their permission.

Changes

We may update this policy when functionality changes, particularly before introducing TikTok login or publishing capabilities. The effective date will be updated accordingly.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the project operator at info@cristianweb.ro.
